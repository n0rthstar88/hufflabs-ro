# World round 5: missing item art

`install-art.py <downloads> <client> <grf list>` installs small rathena.org art packs. It only **adds**
files: anything the client already has (in data.grf or loose) is skipped, so official files are never
overwritten. That fills in sprites and icons for official items the client never had art for (bubble
gums, battle manuals, bullets, card pictures, Domovoi and Wootan Defender loot and more).

Three replacements are deliberate: the HD 8-bit damage numbers, the Poring Cart as cart 1 and the Mini
Tank (by dan) as cart 5.

Skipped: packs identical to the 2025 client, ones that replace official outfits, palette packs (round 4
has its own dyes), an old GPF archive and a mount the client can't use.
